FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What platform do you design websites on?

I design websites using Canva Website and Systeme.io. Both options are beginner friendly, easy to manage, and perfect for service providers, freelancers, coaches, virtual assistants, and online business owners.

Do I need any design or tech skills?

Not at all! Your website will be delivered ready to use, and you'll receive access so you can make simple edits whenever you need to.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. If you'd like something tailored to your brand, audience, and goals, the Cute Package and Power Package are designed for custom projects.

How long does the process take?

Most projects are completed within 3 to 10 business days, depending on the package you choose and how quickly content is provided.

What's included in the free support period?

Every website package includes 30 days of free tech support for minor questions, guidance, and troubleshooting after your website goes live.

What if I'm not ready for a custom website yet?

No worries! You can start with one of my ready made templates and upgrade to a custom design whenever you're ready. It's a great way to launch quickly while keeping things budget friendly.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes! I can help connect your website to your custom domain so your brand looks professional and easy to find online.

Can I edit my website after delivery?

Absolutely. You'll have full access to your website and can update text, images, links, and content whenever you like.

Do you offer branding services too?

Yes! You can add a Custom Branding Suite that includes a wordmark logo, submark, color palette, and curated font selections for a cohesive brand look.
